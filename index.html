Project overview

Silent Hill 4: Sacrament Edition is my take on an expansion for The Room on PC. Of the four Silent Hill games Team Silent made, I’ve always felt this one had the most potential. It did something different. It had an amazing story, some great level design, and a dark, gloomy setting that’s stuck with me.

I truly believe Team Silent had something bigger planned for Silent Hill 4: The Room. I think they wanted to go deeper into the stories of Walter’s first ten victims, the ones who came before Cynthia, Jasper, Andrew, and Richard. I don’t think they had enough development time to show us everything they had in mind.

The clunky combat and unfinished mechanics kept getting in the way of that story for me. That’s where I could feel how rushed things were for Team Silent. I want to bring back the missing, unused, and unreleased content scattered throughout the game, and I want to give those mechanics the time I think they needed.

I’ve been working on this passion project for a long time. My goal is to bring that world back to life and make The Room feel like the polished, fleshed-out Silent Hill title I always thought it could be. When you play it, I hope you, the fans of Silent Hill, can see Team Silent’s vision through my eyes.
